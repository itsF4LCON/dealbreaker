Dealbreaker

A card game for friends where the special cards start a mini-game. Win the duel, or draw the cards.

or join a friend

How to play

  1. Everyone gets 7 cards. Play a card that matches the suit or the rank of the card on the pile, or draw one.
  2. Special cards can be played on anything, but never as your last card.
  3. The first player with no cards left wins.
